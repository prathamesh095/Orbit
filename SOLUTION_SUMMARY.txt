ROOT CAUSE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  REGISTRATION              vs              LOGIN
  ─────────────                            ──────
  
  Email: test@email.com     vs     Email: test@email.com  ✓
  Password: "password "     vs     Password: "password "
       ↓                                  ↓
  Hash: hash_abc_10              Hash: (calculation fails)
                                    ↑
                            No trim! Different hash!
  
  Result: ✗ Login fails

  
SOLUTION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  const passwordTrimmed = password.trim();
  
  Now both registration and login normalize passwords:
  
  "password "  → trim() → "password"  → hash_abc_10
  
  Both produce the same hash. ✓ Login works!

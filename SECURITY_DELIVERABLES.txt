VULNERABILITIES IDENTIFIED
──────────────────────────
Total: 14 (3 CRITICAL, 7 HIGH, 4 MEDIUM)

CRITICAL (3):
  1. Session tokens stored in localStorage (XSS vulnerable)
  2. User credentials database in localStorage (direct exposure)
  3. Weak password hashing (non-cryptographic, demo-only implementation)

HIGH (7):
  4. No encryption at rest (plaintext storage)
  5. No input validation (data corruption risk)
  6. IDOR vulnerability (horizontal privilege escalation)
  7. Insecure session cookies (JavaScript readable)
  8. No rate limiting (brute force attacks)
  9. Insecure password reset tokens (account takeover)
  10. No CSRF protection (cross-site forgery)

MEDIUM (4):
  11. File attachment exposure (metadata leakage)
  12. No audit trail (no accountability)
  13. Storage quota not monitored (silent failures)
  14. No data retention policy (unbounded growth)
